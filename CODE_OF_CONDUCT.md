# Code of Conduct

This is a small personal project. Everyone who takes part is expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good intent
- Keep feedback about the code, not the person
- Accept that the maintainer has the final say on direction

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and spam are not tolerated in issues,
pull requests or any other project space.

The maintainer may edit or remove content and block participants who break these rules.
To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer at the email listed on the GitHub profile.
